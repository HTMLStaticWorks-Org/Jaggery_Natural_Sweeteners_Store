FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers regarding our traditional sweetener processing, delivery timelines, storage wisdom, and purity verification.

1. How can I track my online order and what is the typical dispatch schedule?

Every order is freshly packed within 24 to 48 hours directly from our certified organic farm clusters. Upon dispatch, automated SMS and WhatsApp notifications with live courier tracking links are delivered. Metro transit takes 2–4 business days, while all other Indian pincodes arrive within 4–6 business days in climate-protected, tamper-proof packaging.

2. What is the shelf life of unrefined jaggery cubes and granular shakkar?

Because our jaggery is 100% natural with zero chemical preservatives, solid blocks remain in prime condition for up to 12 months when stored in a cool, dry place wrapped in breathable cloth or palm kottans. Granular shakkar and country sugars have a shelf life of 8 to 10 months when kept in our resealable multi-barrier kraft pouches away from kitchen steam.

3. Do you accommodate bulk wholesale supplies and corporate festive gift boxes?

Yes. We offer commercial wholesale pricing for orders of 25kg or more for bakeries, confectionery makers, restaurants, and Ayurvedic pharmacies. For festive seasons, weddings, and corporate celebrations, we handcraft custom gift hampers in traditional palm-leaf kottans with custom company or family branding.

4. How should I store natural sweeteners during humid monsoon or coastal weather?

Unrefined jaggery naturally absorbs atmospheric humidity. In humid or coastal conditions, transfer blocks and powders to airtight, clean glass mason jars. Never use a damp or wet spoon. For prolonged monsoon storage, keeping sealed glass jars in a cool pantry or refrigerator crisper prevents softening and melting.

5. Are your jaggery products certified 100% chemical-free and unbleached?

Yes, absolutely. Commercial yellow jaggery is bleached with sodium hydrosulphite and synthetic dyes. Amritam jaggery is clarified exclusively using organic wild okra plant mucilage and slow-boiled over woodfire cauldrons, preserving its rich mahogany-golden color, complex caramel notes, and essential plant minerals.
