OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

The Story of AMRITAM: Pure Nectar of Life.

The name AMRITAM comes from the ancient Sanskrit word "Amrita" — the divine nectar of life. We founded Amritam to replace chemical-bleached white sugar with 100% pure, nutrient-dense jaggery handcrafted by Indian farmers using ancestral woodfire methods.

THE CRAFT OF HONEST FOOD

Why We Revived Traditional Indian Jaggery Making

Industrial white sugar is stripped of all natural minerals through chemical bleaching and synthetic clarifiers. At Amritam, we keep the living minerals intact by partnering directly with smallholder farmers in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

Whether it is the early dawn harvest of palmyra neera by generational climbers or the patient boiling of organic cane juice over dried bagasse flames, our products preserve every nuance of terroir, aroma, and natural nutrition.

Ancestral Methods Open iron woks & bagasse-fueled hearths.
Uncompromising Purity No sulfur, no hydros, no artificial colouring.
