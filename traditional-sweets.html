HERITAGE CULINARY SKILL

The Sacred Art of Slow Roasting

Sweet making with jaggery is fundamentally distinct from commercial syrup cooking. Jaggery requires gentle simmering to avoid bitterness, allowing roasted native flours to absorb the golden liquid without becoming heavy or dense.

String Consistency Check: Artisans drop hot syrup into cool water, testing for soft-ball or hard-crack stages by hand.
Aromatic Infusions: Hand-crushed green cardamom, dry ginger powder, and nutmeg folded in during the final cooldown.
