WHY CHOOSE AMRITAM

Rooted in Tradition: Why Natural Sweeteners Are Better for You

True sweetness is not made in industrial refineries. White sugar is stripped of all living minerals through bleaching and chemical clarification. Amritam retains live iron, magnesium, and natural enzymes straight from organic Indian farms.

1
Organic Farm Harvesting

Matured cane and palm sap hand-cut at peak natural sweetness without synthetic pesticides.

2
Gentle Juice Extraction

Pressed immediately in single-pass rollers to preserve live enzymes and raw aroma.

3
Open-Pan Woodfire Clarification

Simmered in giant iron cauldrons clarified naturally using wild okra and plant mucilage.

4
Molding & Hand-Finishing

Poured into handcrafted timber molds or cooled into golden crystalline powder.

HEALTHY DAILY HABITS

How to Replace White Sugar in Daily Cooking & Beverages

Seamlessly integrate Amritam pure sweeteners into your morning filter coffee, spiced chai, porridge, and wholesome baking.

Morning Spiced Chai with Natural Jaggery

Brew black tea with fresh crushed ginger root, cracked green cardamom, and a single cinnamon quill. Stir in raw jaggery powder at the final boil for a comforting, warming cup that never curdles.

Breakfast Porridge & Granola: Sprinkle 1 tablespoon of country sugar over warm oats or ragi malt.
Everyday Cooking & Sambar: Add a thumbnail-sized cube of cane jaggery to balance sour tamarind notes.
